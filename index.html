Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Celsius to Fahrenheit does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app performs temperature conversions entirely on your device and requires no account, login, or permissions.

1

Introduction

Celsius to Fahrenheit ("the App") is a simple temperature conversion tool developed for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2

Information We Collect

✓

We do not collect any information from you — not even anonymous usage data.

The App does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, or any other personal information. There is no sign-up, login, or user profile.

3

How the App Works

On-Device Conversion Only

All temperature conversions are calculated locally on your device. No input is transmitted anywhere.

No Stored Data

The App does not save your conversion history, entries, or any other data. Each session starts fresh.

4

Data Sharing

✓

We do not sell, share, rent, or transmit any data to any third party.

Since the App does not collect any data, there is nothing to share or disclose.

5

Internet Usage

The App works fully offline and does not require an internet connection. No data is sent over the internet by the App.

6

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. No external services receive information about you through the App.

7

Permissions Used

The App does not request any device permissions. It does not access your storage, camera, microphone, location, contacts, or any other sensitive resource.

8

Data Retention & Deletion

Because the App does not collect or store any data, there is no data to retain or delete. Uninstalling the App removes it entirely from your device.

9

Children's Privacy

The App is safe for users of all ages. Since we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly or unknowingly collect data from children under the age of 13.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date at the top. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Questions or Concerns?

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, feel free to reach out.
